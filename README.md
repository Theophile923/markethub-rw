# MarketHub RW Plus
MarketHub RW Plus is a Pi-powered super app designed to integrate multiple mobile-based services into a single digital ecosystem. The platform will initially launch with local passenger transportation services across Rwanda, covering urban areas, rural communities, and cross-border routes with neighboring countries. Over time, MarketHub RW Plus is intended to expand progressively into additional modules including local commerce, tourism, local and regional goods transportation, education, and other community-oriented digital services.

Payments within the MarketHub RW Plus ecosystem are intended to be conducted exclusively in Pi Coin, following the Global Consensus Value (GCV) principle currently referenced by part of the Pi community at 314,159 USD per Pi, while recognizing that the practical transactional value of Pi may evolve over time according to real ecosystem conditions, community agreements, market realities, and future developments within the Pi Network ecosystem.

Rwanda will serve as the pilot country for the initial deployment of MarketHub RW Plus. Following this phase, the platform is intended to expand progressively into other countries where Pi-based payments are accepted, while adapting its services, payment practices, and commercial operations to the economic conditions, legal frameworks, and commercial policies of each country.
